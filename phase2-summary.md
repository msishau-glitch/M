## Final direction
The master visual direction should be a refined ivory background with champagne-gold ornamental framing, delicate botanical accents, and an elegant central portrait placeholder. This gives the invitation a premium editorial wedding tone while staying clean, mobile-friendly, and animation-ready.

## Locked design system to use for later phases
- Background: ivory / warm neutral
- Accent: champagne gold
- Supporting accent: deep burgundy only sparingly
- Decorative style: restrained botanical and fine ornamental linework
- Photo frame: elegant oval or soft-rounded premium frame
- Layout: clean vertical 9:16, strong central focal point
- Motion: slow, soft, premium transition design
- Mobile reading: centered composition with generous whitespace

This is the selected master visual direction for the upcoming final invitation production phase.
